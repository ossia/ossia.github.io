## The ossia.io association

ossia.io is a non-profit association (*association loi 1901*) founded in 2020 in Talence, near Bordeaux, France; it develops and distributes ossia score and libossia, free and open-source software for writing interactive and intermedia works.
In Montréal, a Canadian non-profit organization pursues the same goals.

## Mission

The purpose of the association, as declared in the French *Journal officiel* in 2020, is:

> in France and abroad, the promotion of intermedia artistic creation, in particular through the development and distribution of free digital creative tools; sharing artistic and digital know-how, training and advising on the making of intermedia and interactive works; supporting, contributing to, developing and taking part in an artistic approach for all audiences.

## Activities

The association develops [ossia score](/score/about.html), a sequencer for interactive shows, installations and music, and [libossia](/site-libossia/about.html), a library which connects creative environments such as Max, Pure Data, SuperCollider, openFrameworks or Unity; both are free and open-source, and run on Windows, macOS, Linux, Raspberry Pi and in the web browser ([download](/score/download.html)).
The [documentation](https://ossia.io/score-docs/), tutorials and examples are published online, and members of the association give workshops and courses in schools, universities and art centers ([teaching and workshops](/teaching.html)).

ossia is used by artists and companies of the performing arts, composers, museums and exhibition designers, teachers and researchers; for instance, the electronics of Pía Baltazar's *The Ripples of Consequence* (Le Vivier, Montréal, 2025) are run by ossia score, and other works are presented in the [gallery](/gallery.html).

The software comes from the research on interactive scores started in 2006 with the LaBRI and the SCRIME in Bordeaux ([project history](/project.html)); the ossia collective also took part in the definition of the OSCQuery protocol, which is used to discover and control media software over the network.

## Funding

The work of the association is funded by donations on [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/ossia), by the sale of ossia score on the Microsoft Store (the same version is a free download on this website), and by partnerships with cultural and research organizations such as the [Société des arts technologiques](https://sat.qc.ca/) (SAT) in Montréal.
The research projects which led to ossia score were funded by the French National Research Agency (ANR) and the French Ministry of Culture.
