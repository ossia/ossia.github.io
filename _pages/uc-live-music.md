ossia score is used for concerts, live electronics and audiovisual performances. A piece can be written precisely in time,
improvised around loops and patterns, or anything in between, and the visuals and lights follow the same clock.

## What you can do

- **Instruments included**: a multi-format sampler (SF2, GIG, DLS, Hydrogen drum kits), drum machines, an FM synthesizer, a polyphonic granulator, Synthimi, Bytebeat, and Faust physical models ([synths](https://ossia.io/score-docs/examples/audio/synths.html), [granular](https://ossia.io/score-docs/examples/audio/granular.html)).
- **Patterns and sequencing**: step sequencers with accents and slides, 303 and drum pattern libraries, a piano roll, an arpeggiator, MIDI humanize and quantize, and Euclidean rhythms from JavaScript ([patternist](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/patternist.html), [arpeggiator](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/arpeggiator.html)).
- **Tempo everywhere**: tempo and time signatures, automation that snaps to the musical grid, bar-locked LFOs, a looper quantized to bars, and a beat tracker ([musical features](https://ossia.io/score-docs/in-depth/musical.html), [looper](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/audio-looper.html)).
- **Sync**: generate or follow MIDI clock and MIDI timecode, follow LTC, and control the timeline with a timecode vinyl on a turntable ([MIDI sync](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/midi-sync.html), [timecode vinyl](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/xwax-dvs.html)).
- **Plug-ins and effects**: VST, VST3, CLAP (with sample-accurate automation), LV2 and JSFX hosting; the Airwindows effects, Faust, and built-in compressor, limiter, reverbs, filters and delays ([audio plug-ins](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/audio-plugins.html), [audio effects](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/audio-effects.html)).
- **Microtonal music** with Scala scales and keyboard mappings ([scales](https://ossia.io/score-docs/examples/audio/scales.html)).
- **Controllers**: MIDI controllers with named knobs and faders, MCU-compatible control surfaces, network MIDI, MIDI 2, wind controllers, tablets and gamepads.
- **Audiovisual**: audio-reactive shaders, 3D and video, and DMX lights driven by the music (see [video](/projection-mapping.html) and [lighting](/dmx-lighting.html)).
