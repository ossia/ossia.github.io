ossia score is used in classes and workshops on interactive sound, video, lighting and installation, for instance at Concordia University, UQAM, the University of Ottawa, Queen's University (Kingston, Ontario), Maynooth University and the Université de Bordeaux; the sound and lighting students of ENSATT in Lyon also used it for *Dô d'eau* (2023), with spatialized sound, video and DMX lighting.
Since it is free and runs on every operating system, students can install it on their own computers and keep their projects after the course.

## Getting started

The [browser version](https://ossia.io/score-web) does not require any installation: it supports the camera, the microphone, MIDI controllers, gamepads and OSC, and opens the examples and zipped projects directly.
Once installed, score runs on Windows (x64 and ARM), macOS, Linux and the Raspberry Pi, with the same project files and the same features, except those which depend on a given system or piece of hardware (Spout, Syphon, CUDA, etc.) ([embedded](https://ossia.io/score-docs/in-depth/embedded.html)).

A library of example scores covers audio, video, 3D, AI, devices and data, and each object links to its documentation ([examples](https://ossia.io/score-docs/examples.html), [quick start](https://ossia.io/score-docs/quick-start.html)).

## During a course

Students can start with cables and timelines and then use JavaScript, Faust, shaders or C++ in the same project ([JavaScript](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/javascript.html), [shaders](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/shaders.html), [Faust](https://ossia.io/score-docs/processes/faust.html)).
Sound and synthesis, video and 3D, DMX lighting, sensors and Arduino, computer vision and AI are available in the same software, so that a class project can combine them.

For hardware kits, a Raspberry Pi runs a finished project without a screen, with GPIO for electronics ([headless mode](https://ossia.io/score-docs/faq/nogui.html)); the editor also comes with about thirty skins, including color-blind-safe and high-contrast ones.

Other works are presented in the [gallery](/gallery.html), where the "Education" filter shows student projects.
