## Contact

The association can be reached at **[contact@ossia.io](mailto:contact@ossia.io)**, for instance for partnerships, workshops and training, artistic projects, institutions and press.

## Questions about the software

Questions about using ossia score and libossia are best asked where the community and the developers can answer them:

- the [forum](https://forum.ossia.io), for questions and for sharing projects;
- the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/8Hzm4UduaS), to discuss with other users and the developers;
- the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/ossia/score/issues), to report a bug or propose a feature.

The [documentation](https://ossia.io/score-docs/) answers many questions, in particular its [troubleshooting](https://ossia.io/score-docs/troubleshooting.html) page.

## Follow the project

News and works made with ossia are posted on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ossia_io/), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ossia.io), [X](https://x.com/ossia_io), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCwghQysyNdstRfv8YUPyglw) and [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/ossia); the source code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ossia).
