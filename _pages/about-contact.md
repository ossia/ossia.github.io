## Contact

The association can be reached at **[contact@ossia.io](mailto:contact@ossia.io)**, for instance for partnerships, workshops, artistic projects or press.

## Questions about the software

Questions about using ossia score and libossia can be asked on the [forum](https://forum.ossia.io) or on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/8Hzm4UduaS); bugs and feature requests go to the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/ossia/score/issues).
The [documentation](https://ossia.io/score-docs/) also has a [troubleshooting](https://ossia.io/score-docs/troubleshooting.html) page.

## Social networks

News and works made with ossia are posted on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ossia_io/), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ossia.io), [X](https://x.com/ossia_io), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCwghQysyNdstRfv8YUPyglw) and [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/ossia); the source code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ossia).
