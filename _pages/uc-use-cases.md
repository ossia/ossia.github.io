ossia score is an intermedia sequencer: sound, video, 3D, lights, sensors and any OSC or MIDI device share the same timeline, which can also wait for events, branch and loop.
Many projects only need score, from the first sketches to the performances; when other software or hardware is already in place, score can also drive it.

## About ossia score

ossia score is free and open-source (GPLv3), with no feature or resolution limit, and runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, the Raspberry Pi and embedded Linux, as well as [in the browser](https://ossia.io/score-web).
Triggers, conditions and branches are part of the timeline itself, and score communicates with other software over OSC, OSCQuery, MIDI, DMX, NDI, Spout and Syphon, with plug-ins and Pure Data patches running inside it.
It comes from the research on interactive scores done at LaBRI and SCRIME with the *i-score* project; see the [project history](/project.html), and the [gallery](/gallery.html) for works made with it.
