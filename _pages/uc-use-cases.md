ossia score is an intermedia sequencer: sound, video, 3D, lights, sensors and any OSC or MIDI device share one timeline, and that timeline can wait, branch and loop.
Plenty of projects need nothing else, from the first sketch to the last show. When you already have other software or hardware, score drives it too.

## About ossia score

It is free and open-source (GPLv3), with no feature or resolution limits, and runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Raspberry Pi and embedded Linux, and [in the browser](https://ossia.io/score-web).
Triggers, conditions and branching are part of the timeline itself, and it talks to other tools over OSC, OSCQuery, MIDI, DMX, NDI, Spout and Syphon, with plug-ins and Pure Data patches inside.
It has been developed for more than ten years with research labs and artists: see the [project history](/project.html) and the [gallery](/gallery.html).
